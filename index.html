a quieter way to meet.

No infinite feed. No public broadcast. Just a soft signal of who's around — and the option to say so.

01 / nearby

feel the room.

See a calm, ambient view of people who've also gone live nearby. Distance is the only metric. No follower counts, no scores, no scroll.

02 / interest

send privately.

Tap a dot to send quiet interest. Nothing is shown publicly. The other person only learns you're interested if they're interested too.

03 / mutual

meet in person.

When it's mutual, Radial introduces you with one short note. The rest happens in the room you're both already in.
